# Audience profiles by age

The user picks an age band in the brief (`קבוצת גיל`). Load the matching profile before writing
concepts. Profiles are starting points, not stereotypes — the brief's real audience wins.
If the audience spans bands, write for the **decision maker** (e.g. parents for kids' products).

Codes: `kids` · `teens` · `young` · `adults` · `parents` · `mature` · `seniors`

---

## kids — 6–12 (the ad is really for the parent)
- **Watches:** YouTube, YouTube Kids, TikTok via older siblings. Short, colorful, loud.
- **Humor:** slapstick, silly sounds, absurd characters, grown-ups being wrong.
- **Twist style:** magic reveal, toy/animal "comes alive", kid outsmarts adult.
- **Pace:** fast, bright colors, big rounded fonts (Varela Round, Fredoka).
- **Address:** speak to the parent in copy; show the kid's joy on screen.
- **Hard rules:** see `legal-il.md` → minors. No "ask your parents to buy", no pressure, no
  exploiting credulity, no unsafe imitable behavior.

## teens — 13–17
- **Watches:** TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts; sound-on trends, memes, creators.
- **Values:** belonging, identity, not looking "cringe", humor, authenticity, independence.
- **Humor:** meta, self-aware, absurdist, meme formats, fast irony.
- **Twist style:** fake-out of an ad format ("זו לא עוד פרסומת…"), POV that turns, deadpan.
- **Pace:** cuts every 1–1.5 s, text-heavy, trending audio patterns (licensed only).
- **Avoid:** adults imitating teen slang, preaching, anything that feels like a teacher talking.
- **Rules:** minors — same restrictions as kids for purchase pressure; be careful with body image.

## young — 18–30
- See `youth-tone.md` for tone detail.
- **Moments:** army/national service ending, studies, first job, first apartment, travel, dating.
- **Values:** authenticity, meaning, flexibility, money-smart, community.
- **Humor:** relatable "POV", irony, understatement, internet culture.
- **Twist style:** expectation flip, relatable scenario with an unexpected turn, chat simulations.
- **Pace:** 1.5–2.5 s cuts, UGC look, captions always.

## adults — 30–45 (working, busy)
- **Watches:** Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp status; often in short gaps.
- **Values:** time, competence, family balance, value for money, quality.
- **Humor:** situational, dry, "so true" moments from work and home life.
- **Twist style:** everyday chaos → one smart fix; reversal of a familiar scene; understatement.
- **Pace:** medium (2–3 s shots); clarity beats frenzy. Proof and specifics matter.

## parents — parents of young kids (typically 28–45)
- **Watches:** Facebook groups, Instagram, WhatsApp; late night or naptime scrolling.
- **Values:** child's safety and happiness, trust, convenience, feeling like a good parent.
- **Humor:** parenting chaos, tired-parent truths, kids saying unexpected things.
- **Twist style:** "who's really being taken care of here?" — the parent turns out to be the hero
  or beneficiary; kid's-eye view that reveals the adult truth.
- **Trust signals:** real staff, real place, certifications, other parents' voices.
- **Rules:** no identifiable kids' faces without consent; never shame parents.

## mature — 45–64
- **Watches:** Facebook, YouTube (longer), WhatsApp forwards, TV-style content.
- **Values:** reliability, experience, health, family, respect, not being talked down to.
- **Humor:** warm, nostalgic (80s–90s Israeli references), gentle irony about getting older — never mocking.
- **Twist style:** nostalgia flip (then vs now), role reversal with adult children, quiet surprise.
- **Pace:** 3–4 s shots, larger text (+15%), slower VO (~130 wpm), high contrast.
- **Show** people their age as capable and active, not as a punchline.

## seniors — 65+
- **Watches:** Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp videos shared by family; TV.
- **Values:** independence, dignity, connection with grandchildren, health, security, clarity.
- **Humor:** warm, wise, grandparent wit — the senior is the smart one in the room.
- **Twist style:** the senior outsmarts the young ones; tech-savvy reveal; heartfelt turn.
- **Pace:** 4+ s shots, very large text (captions ≥ 72 px), clear VO (~120 wpm), minimal
  motion blur/flashing, strong contrast, one message only.
- **Avoid:** frailty stereotypes, fear-mongering, tiny legal text, fast-cut chaos.
