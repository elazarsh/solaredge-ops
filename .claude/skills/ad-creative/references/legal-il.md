# Israeli compliance checklist (not legal advice — flag, don't decide)

## Job / recruitment ads — חוק שוויון ההזדמנויות בעבודה, סעיף 8
- A job ad must address **both genders** and must not discriminate by gender, age, religion,
  nationality, origin, personal status, parenthood, pregnancy, sexual orientation, etc.
- Options: dual form ("דרושים/ות", "מחפשים מטפלים ומטפלות"), neutral wording ("מחפשים צוות"),
  or feminine copy **plus a visible note**: "המודעה מנוסחת בלשון נקבה אך מיועדת לכל המינים".
  In a video, put that note on the CTA end card (small but readable, ≥ 2 s) and in the post text.
- Visuals/targeting should not signal "only young women" — avoid "צעירות בלבד", age limits, etc.
- Violations can bring administrative fines per ad. Always tell the user when their script is gendered.

## Consumer protection / misleading advertising
- Every claim must be true and provable ("שכר הכי גבוה באזור" needs proof — prefer concrete facts).
- Prices/conditions: don't hide material conditions in tiny text.

## People and images
- **Children:** no identifiable faces without written parental consent. Prefer hands, backs,
  blurred or illustrated children.
- Staff/testimonials: written consent for face, name and quote.
- Don't use real people's likeness or voice (celebs, politicians) without permission; no fake testimonials.

## Ads aimed at minors (kids / teens)
- Consumer-protection and broadcast ad rules restrict advertising to minors: no direct call on
  children to buy or to pressure parents ("תבקשו מאמא"), no exploiting inexperience or credulity,
  no depicting dangerous behavior they might copy, no implying the child is inferior without the product.
- Address purchase messages to parents; keep prices/conditions clear.
- Twists for kids must not frighten (no scary fake-outs) — magic and silliness, not horror.

## Music, fonts, footage
- Only licensed or royalty-free music; trending sounds in organic posts ≠ licensed for paid ads.
- Fonts in `video-studio/remotion/public/fonts` are open-licensed (OFL) and fine for commercial use.
- AI-generated images of "real" staff/place presented as real is misleading — label or avoid.

## Platform ad policies
- Meta "Special Ad Category: Employment" applies to job ads: limited targeting (no age/gender targeting).
  Plan creative that self-selects the audience instead of relying on targeting.
