# 100 camera movement codes

**Dolly (approach/retreat, 1-10):** Slow Dolly In, Fast Dolly In, Crash Dolly In, Slow Dolly Out, Fast Dolly Out, Dolly-In Reveal, Dolly-Out Reveal, Ground-Level Dolly In, Dolly In Zoom Out, Dolly Out Zoom In

**Pan/Tilt (fixed position, 11-20):** Slow Pan Left, Slow Pan Right, Fast Pan Left, Fast Pan Right, Pan to Reveal, Follow Pan, Tilt Up, Tilt Down, Slow Tilt-Up Reveal, Slow Tilt-Down Reveal

**Tracking/Truck (lateral, 21-30):** Track from Behind, Track from the Front, Side Tracking, Over-the-Shoulder Tracking, Low-Level Tracking, Truck Left, Truck Right, Lateral Reveal, Parallel Vehicle Tracking, Tracking Through a Crowd

**Arc/Orbit (31-40):** Clockwise Arc, Counterclockwise Arc, Quarter Orbit, Half Orbit, Full Orbit, Slow Product Orbit, Low-Angle Orbit, Rising Spiral Orbit, Descending Spiral Orbit, Lazy Susan Rotation

**Zoom (41-50):** Slow Zoom In, Slow Zoom Out, Crash Zoom In, Crash Zoom Out, Zoom to a Detail, Zoom-Out Context Reveal, Yo-Yo Zoom, Infinite Zoom, Super Zoom, Stepped Zoom

**Roll/Whip (51-60):** Clockwise Camera Roll, Counterclockwise Camera Roll, Half Camera Roll, Full Camera Roll, Slow Rolling Push-In, Rolling Pull-Out, Whip Pan Left, Whip Pan Right, Whip Pan Transition, Whip Pan to a Stop

**Pedestal/FPV (61-70):** Pedestal Up, Pedestal Down, Rising Reveal, Descending Reveal, Vertical Rise and Hold, Low FPV Flythrough, FPV Dive, FPV Through a Window, FPV Chase, FPV Slalom

**Handheld/Attached (71-80):** Subtle Handheld, Documentary Handheld, Handheld Walk, Handheld Run, Handheld Push-In, Camera Attached to a Suitcase / Bicycle / Car Door / Tool / Rotating Object

**Locked-On/Snorricam/Double Dolly (81-90):** Face / Eye / Product / Hand / Foot Locked-On, Walking / Running / Spinning Snorricam, Forward Double Dolly, Sideways Double Dolly

**Bullet time / Speed ramp (91-100):** Frozen-Moment Orbit, Bullet-Time Half Orbit, Bullet-Time Push-In, Bullet-Time Pull-Out, Bullet Time into Real Time, Fast-to-Slow Ramp, Slow-to-Fast Ramp, Fast-Slow-Fast Ramp, Speed-Ramped Orbit, Speed-Ramped Flythrough

Visual references for each family exist in the Eyecandy library (eyecannndy.com). Use it to learn movement families, not as licence for commercial reuse.
