# PROJECT STATE — «בין כוח לברכה» (Parashat Toldot)

_Durable summary of decisions. Claude sessions are ephemeral; this file lets any session continue. Update it when decisions change._

## What the film is
- Title: **«בין כוח לברכה»**. 9:16 vertical (WhatsApp status). ~4:20 = 3 s intro + 4:15 narration + 3 s end card.
- Message: leadership is a **crown and a fist**. Healthy power (crown and splendor: respect, service, responsibility, listening) vs ugly violent domination (silencing, erasing, controlling another's pay). The film follows the hand: open or closed.
- **Brother vs enemy:** legitimate disagreement stays inside a thin **gold ring** around the round table (Isaac–Rebecca; the dissenting brother at the end). Violence and refusal to accept the other (Esau's "וְאַהַרְגָה") break the ring; that chair stays outside. The final chair pulled into the light is a **different** chair (the green dissenting brother).
- Recurring motifs: crown-shadow = fist (end: open hand) · the gold ring · two different chairs · hands.

## Decisions (do not re-open without the user)
- Style **C**: paper-cut collage, riso print, **alive** (stop-motion jitter, grain, halftone, torn-paper/ink transitions, voice-line animation, kinetic Hebrew type). Faceless by design (top-down, from behind, shadows, hands).
- Narration: **the first Google (Gemini TTS) recording, male voice, original pace approved** → `voice/audio/narr_v1_original.mp3` (4:15). Text locked to what was recorded: `narr.md` (two changes vs the first draft: end of P6 = «ההחלטה באמת קשה.»; end of P9 = «גם את מי שרואה את הסכנה»). Older text kept in `voice/narr_v1_locked_before_recording.md`.
- Two niqqud typos in the recording text to listen for: «הָהַחלַטָה בֶּאֵמֵת קָשַה», «הָסַכָּנָה» (should be הַהַחְלָטָה בֶּאֱמֶת קָשָׁה / הַסַּכָּנָה).
- **No paid tool/API calls without the user's prior approval** (monthly cap). Images are produced by the user's GPT (this `art/` workflow), the film is assembled by Claude with free tools (HTML/WebGL + ffmpeg).
- **No weapons** shown (Esau has no bow). Sensitive beats are restrained and faceless.
- File delivery: one MP4 (no splitting unless asked), compressed to a reasonable size. End card: credit + AI-transparency line.
