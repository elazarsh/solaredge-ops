# Effects catalog (frame-level, in code)

Adapted from the effect prompts in Brain's "the video that edited itself" guide (brainai.co.il/guides/claude-video-edited-itself), rewritten as parameters. Reusable on any footage. Numbers are starting points, not laws; tune by looking at frames.

**How to use:** pick the effect, bind it to a word (original time `t0` from the word table), convert
with `TimeMap.to_final`, render only the frames in its window, check with `video-qa`.

Legend - Needs: **M** person mask, **P** clean plate (background without the person, via
`cv2.inpaint` on the mask), **W** perspective warp (`cv2.warpPerspective`), **G** graphics only.
Common finishers (reuse): short white flash (2-3 frames), decaying camera shake, RGB split
(shift red and blue channels a few pixels opposite ways), glow (blurred additive copy).

| 1 | Grey to colour slam (opener) | G | until the word, then 0.15 s | Before the word: desaturated video, flat dark background, no glow, small grey "unedited" tag, slow ~10% push-in. On the word: colour returns in 0.15 s, animated background and frame glow switch on, title lands from 145% to 100% with a small overshoot and slight rotation, tag flips to its new text, CTA slides up; strong decaying shake + white flash + RGB split. A red underline stretches in after landing; exit scales up ~3x and vanishes in 0.14 s; small shake on landing. |
| 3 | Screen cracks and shatters | G | crack 0.15 s, shatter, ~0.5 s, reassemble | ~50 Voronoi cells (`cv2.Subdiv2D`), half the seeds clustered near the centre. Cell borders draw in white spreading outward just before the burst. Each cell becomes a shard with a bright edge, flies away from the centre with gravity, rotation and growth; teal glow from the centre, strong shake, flash. After ~0.5 s run it in reverse: shards fly home and the picture is whole again. |
| 4 | Step out of the frame | M | in 0.33 s, hold ~1 s, back 0.16 s | Scale the masked person up to ~1.8x with a small overshoot, anchored at the bottom edge of the frame so head and shoulders rise above the frame and over the title; everything below the frame bottom is clipped. Teal rim light and soft drop shadow. |
| 5 | Whole reel flips in 3D | W | half turn 0.45 s, hold upside-down 0.33 s, half turn back | Render the full frame first, then map it onto a quad rotating about the horizontal axis with real perspective at ~94% size over a clean background; whoosh on each half turn. Captions and title flip with it. |

Add an expanding white shockwave, particles frozen mid-air with short trails, a stopwatch icon that pops above the frame, caption slightly tilted. On release: flash + RGB split. |
| 8 | Become a giant | M | jumps on the beat | Background swaps to a miniature city (image). Soft shadow below, bottom fade so the body is not cut by a hard line. After ~1 s snap back. |
| 9 | Dissolve to pixels and rebuild | M + P | out ~0.6 s, hold, in ~0.6 s | Clean plate behind. Sample the person into 12 px blocks; each block gets a release time from a delay map (left-to-right and bottom-to-top plus noise). Released blocks drift up-right on a small wave, grow, fade and shift to teal. Short hold when almost gone, then the reverse. The caption pixelates too. |
| 10 | Infinite zoom into the screen | G | ~1 s per dive, 3 levels | A phone appears in the frame playing the reel itself (recursively, three levels deep). On each chosen word the camera dives into the phone screen over ~1 s. Use exponential scale (`scale = k ** progress`) so speed feels constant, aim at the screen centre, add radial zoom blur mid-dive, and redraw the inner screen from a sharp render at the right size so the end of the dive is not blurry. Captions fade during the dive. |
| 11 | Rotating cube with 4 cards | G | 0.3 s per 90 deg turn | Frame becomes a cube; show two faces in perspective during each turn. Each face carries a short animated card demonstrating a use case (e.g. product flies in with "NEW" and a red sale tag; a feed scrolls with motion blur and stops with a ring; an invitation whose text is made of particles that fly in and settle; slides rising in perspective with a growing bar chart). The speaker's face stays in a round bubble bottom corner with a teal ring. |
| 12 | Price stamps and counter to zero | G | stamps 0.12 s each | Two red stamps drop from 2.6x to 1x in 0.12 s, tilted, with a tiny shake. On the key word a red strike line is drawn through each and they fall off-screen with gravity and spin. Three tags pop ("prompt 1/2/3"). A boxed counter falls from the old amount to 0 in 0.5 s with ease-out; at zero it turns teal and a tag pops. |
| 13 | Comment typed, DM arrives | G | typing + 0.5 s + slide | A white social-style comment box slides in with the speaker's round avatar. The keyword types letter by letter with a blinking cursor, the send button presses, a red heart pops. 0.5 s later a DM notification slides from the top with the logo; the CTA button grows, pulses and gets a light sweep. Draw it yourself in the app's style, never paste real app screenshots. |
| 14 | Hologram reveal | M | top-to-bottom wipe | A line of light travels down the frame; above it the person is already a hologram: teal tones by brightness, moving scanlines, glowing Canny edges inside the mask, slight flicker and colour-channel offset; dark grid background. Three tags pop one after another, targeting brackets around the face; flash at the end returns to normal. |

## Placement rules

- Anchor to the **original** time of the word; let `TimeMap` convert. Never type final-video seconds.
- One big effect per ~3-5 seconds at most; leave air between them.
- When two effects overlap, draw order: background < clean plate < person < graphics < captions < flashes.
- Skip an effect that does not fit the footage, and say which and why.
- Every effect must be checked on frames at its peak and one frame after it ends (no residue).
