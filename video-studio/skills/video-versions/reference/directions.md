# Video directions menu

Pick five rows that differ from each other. Adapt every choice to the subject's own world: its
materials, places, tools and vocabulary are where distinctive choices come from.

| # | Direction | Structure | Look | Pacing | Sound world | Signature moment idea |

| 1 | Cold-open proof | Result first, then how | Clean, one brand colour, big numerals | Fast | Music-driven, hits on numbers | A counter that lands exactly on the claim |

## Two rules for the signature moment

Every version gets one thing a viewer will remember, and only one. Keep everything around it calm.
Examples: a price counter that falls to zero, a shutter that opens on the logo, a word written in
the air in the speaker's own handwriting rhythm.

## Format notes

- Reels/TikTok/Shorts 9:16 at 1080x1920; keep key content inside the safe zone (away from the top
  ~15% and bottom ~20% where the app chrome sits).
- YouTube 16:9 at 1920x1080; first 5 seconds must state the promise.
- Square 1:1 for feeds. Always check captions at phone size.
