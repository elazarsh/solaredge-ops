# Scene blueprints (story-role patterns)

Pick one per beat by role. Each = a short choreography with typical duration. Compose from the technique catalog when none fits. (Condensed from the 22 blueprints of HyperFrames' animation skill, paraphrased.)

| Blueprint | Role(s) | Duration | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinetic type beats | Hook, Problem, Intro, Benefits, CTA | 3-13 s | bold centred/edge type; words ARE the motion; hard-cut token swaps or a statement building in beats to a spring-pop payoff |
| Typewriter reveal | Hook, Outro | 3.6-7 s | caret types and edits a line, collapses to a brand payoff, or types a CTA under a persistent mark |
| Spatial pan stations | Hook, Problem, Intro | 7-10 s | labelled stations on one big canvas; a camera pans station to station and holds on the last |
| Camera journey | Benefits, Feature | 5.6-11 s | multi-leg camera tells the story; (A) click triggers a swoop to its consequence, (B) cursorless 3D flight |
| Zoom-out workspace reveal | Hook, Benefits | 6.8-11 s | open on a tight detail; one decelerating zoom-out reveals the containing workspace |
| Constellation hub | Hook, Social proof, CTA | 5-8 s | nodes spring into a ring around a centre; camera push-in / orbit / collapse into the product |
| Grid card assemble | Feature, Benefits, Proof | 3-10.5 s | items cascade into a grid/list and hold; feature grids, logo walls, live boards |
| Logo assemble lockup | Intro, CTA, Outro | 4.4-11 s | brand mark forms from parts then resolves into a centred lockup + URL/CTA |
| Cursor UI demo | Intro, Feature, Hook | 4-13 s | visible cursor drives a reconstructed UI via clicks/drags; camera chases interactions |
| Device surface showcase | Feature, Intro | 5-11 s | a device/floating window is the hero while screens cycle a real flow |
| Prompt type-submit-generate | Hook, Intro, Feature, CTA | 5-12 s | prompt types into a real input and the product answers (streaming, install command end card) |
| Agent progress theater | Feature | 4-12 s | working-state theatre: checklist checks off row by row, or a conversation builds to confirmation |
| Panel edit live sync | Feature | 5-12 s | inspector control edits update a target surface live on the same beat |
| Transcript scroll -> artifact reveal | Feature | 5-12 s | frame travels down a long transcript/doc as proof of work, then one interaction reveals the deliverable |
| Data viz count-up | Hook, Problem, Intro, Proof | 4-12 s | numbers/charts are the hero; camera pushes through to land on one headline metric |
| Title card reveal | Benefits, Proof, CTA | 3-5 s | calm title/proof card, one restrained move, then held (chain of 2-3 for ~6-9 s) |
| Comparison split | Feature | 4-6 s | two equal items enter from opposite sides with mirrored 3D tilts, then badges pop |
| Overwhelm surround | Problem | 6-10 s | tools accumulate, the centre one morphs into the viewer, elements close in from all sides; alt: clutter pushed to the edges, a serif question builds |
| Ticker takeover | Hook, Outro | 5-7 s | typed lead-in with a cycling accent word is violently replaced when a hero crashes in |
| Fixed-anchor cycle | Hook, Benefits, Outro | 6.6-11 s | one element stays pinned while the region around it cycles through states then resolves into a lockup |
| Video-text pivot | Intro, Feature | 6-8 s | product video slides aside to hand weight to a hero stat, then kinetic text types into the centre |
| CTA morph-press | CTA, Hook | 4-6 s | brand mark condenses into a brighter CTA; a cursor lands the click |

## Role menu
Hook: kinetic type, typewriter, stations, zoom-out reveal, constellation, prompt-submit, ticker takeover, fixed-anchor, CTA-morph. Problem: kinetic type, stations, overwhelm surround, data count-up. Product intro: logo assemble, cursor demo, device showcase, prompt-submit, zoom-out. Key feature: camera journey, cursor demo, device showcase, agent theatre, panel sync, transcript->artifact, comparison split. Benefits: kinetic type, grid assemble, zoom-out, fixed-anchor. Social proof: constellation, grid/logo wall, data count-up, title card. CTA: logo lockup, CTA morph-press, constellation end card, prompt-submit. Outro/brand: logo assemble, typewriter, ticker takeover, fixed-anchor.

## Story spine for narrated pieces
Hook = outcome language ("what you gain/avoid/understand"), numbers only when they carry stakes. Value by beat 2. Storyboard presented as a proposal table: frame | beat+duration | on screen | why (traceable to the message); a frame whose "why" can't be traced is cut. Visual vocabulary mined from the source (its phrases, entities, verbs, numbers). Rhythm declared up front; for a 5-7 beat reel use 1-2 hero transitions. Changes are cheapest at the storyboard stage (30 s there vs minutes after build).

## Format notes
1920x1080 landscape, 1080x1920 portrait, 1080x1080 square. Full-bleed backgrounds on a clip layer, not the root. Duration guides: hook 3-4 s, ad 15-30 s, launch 30-60 s, motion-graphic sting <10 s (up to 30 s).
